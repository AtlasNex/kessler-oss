## What you may do without asking

- **Use the code.** Run it, modify it, ship it inside your own products under Apache-2.0.
- **Nominative reference.** Say your tool "uses Kessler" or "is built on Kessler" and link
  back here. A factual reference is not a trademark use.
- **Localize the character for your fork, or remove it.** If you fork the project you may
  (a) strip every Nex asset and rendering, or (b) replace it with your own character. This
  is the modified-distribution model: forks change the marks. Keep this file in the fork.

## What you may not do without written permission

- **No commerce in the marks.** No merchandise, no product or company names using
  "Kessler" or "Nex", and no "Kessler-certified" or similar implication of endorsement or
  certification.
- **No off-model Nex.** The character rules are part of the brand: no mouth, no redrawn
  poses, no props. If in doubt, do not draw the character; link to this repository instead.
- **No misleading source.** Do not present a modified fork as the official Kessler.

## Why there is a character at all

The one rule behind it: **Nex appears only where a reader is not verifying a measurement.**
The character is fenced off every number, finding and verdict, by design in the upstream
tree (a build-time rule refuses a character asset on any findings surface, and the tests
pin that). The fence is the product. The public version of that story, with the method
behind it, is at
[kessler.atlasnex.com/reports/05-methodology.html](https://kessler.atlasnex.com/reports/05-methodology.html).
